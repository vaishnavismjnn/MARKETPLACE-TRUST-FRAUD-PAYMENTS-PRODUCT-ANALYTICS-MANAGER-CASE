# Marketplace Trust, Fraud & Payments — Product Analytics Case Study

## Project Overview
This project analyzes marketplace payment performance, fraud risk, chargebacks, merchant risk, and customer friction to identify opportunities to improve payment conversion and marketplace trust.

## Key Analyses
- **Payment Funnel Analysis:** Evaluated orders, transaction outcomes, payment success rates, and payment method and gateway performance.
- **Risk Score Analysis:** Segmented transactions into risk bands and analyzed chargeback patterns.
- **False-Decline Analysis:** Identified potentially wrongly declined low-risk transactions and estimated revenue at risk.
- **Chargeback Economics:** Analyzed chargeback counts, amounts, and financial exposure across merchant categories.
- **Merchant Risk Scorecard:** Compared merchant categories using chargeback metrics and risk classifications.
- **Investment Scenario:** Developed a hypothetical ₹18 crore investment allocation across fraud detection, payment infrastructure, customer verification, merchant risk, and customer experience.
- **Executive Recommendations:** Translated analytical findings into recommendations to balance fraud prevention, payment conversion, and customer experience.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Excel:** Data-quality auditing, analytical models, formulas, KPI calculations, and investment analysis.
- **MySQL:** Joins, aggregations, CTEs, window functions, analytical queries, and reusable SQL views.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards for executive KPIs, payment funnels, fraud and chargebacks, merchant risk, and customer friction.

## Business Objective
To use data-driven analysis to understand payment friction and chargeback exposure while supporting informed decisions that balance marketplace security with customer conversion and experience.

## Disclaimer
The ₹18 crore investment allocation is a hypothetical business scenario developed for analytical and recommendation purposes.
